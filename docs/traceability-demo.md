# FR003 traceability record

The requester need is to see the saved progress of an own request without another user’s information. FR-003 maps to AC-FR-003-1 (saved status/date after an authorised update) and AC-FR-003-2 (foreign reference reveals no record), ADR-M2-05/06, RequestQueryService, RequestAccessPolicy, EfRequestReader and RequestStatusService.

M3 E01/E02 established the protected status/read increment under its recorded simulation. M4 P07/P24/P26 extend the trace through real credential sign-in, stored submission and the browser presentation. The final reports retain the source, environment and operator. The same RTM row records the outcome. Public production readiness remains a separate qualified decision in release-evidence.md.
